# Quality rubric for a school concept page (judge's guide)

Judge as an experienced mathematics teacher and textbook editor reading for the page's intended reader (page_spec_v2.md:
a teenager or adult learning this foundation properly). Score each criterion 1-5 (5 = as good as the best textbooks or
expository writing you know; 3 = acceptable but clearly improvable; 1 = fails). Quote the page for every score below 4.

1. Clarity: a reader can follow every step; symbols explained; no jumps; sentences plain and exact.
2. Mathematical substance: precise definitions; key facts justified (why, not only what); correct edge cases (0, 1, empty).
3. Economy: no repetition, no template padding, no lesson-plan narration, no talk about sources; every paragraph teaches.
   Reading time proportionate to the concept.
4. Examples and exercises: few, well chosen, each with a distinct point; exercises range from routine to thoughtful
   (at least one "why"/proof-type item); solutions complete.
5. Insight and connections: at least one idea that makes the concept click (a good picture, a second viewpoint, a
   surprising consequence); "Looking ahead" makes the road to later mathematics visible.
6. Fit for the reader: neither childish nor needlessly abstract; no classroom/teacher advice.
7. Figures: each figure helps and matches the text; none decorative.

Verdict: pass if every criterion >= 4 and the mean >= 4.3; otherwise revise, with the 3-6 most important changes,
each concrete (what to cut, what to add, what to rewrite, where).
